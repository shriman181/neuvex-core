Neuvex Playground

Be the AI agent. Watch Neuvex govern you, live on Neon.

Inboxly is a demo email app on Neuvex. It lets AI agents use three primitives, each under a contract stored in Neon Postgres. Pick a scenario and send it: the real Neuvex gateway reads the contract from Neon, cuts the data down to what you may see, and appends its decision to an audit log in Neon.
